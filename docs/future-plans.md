This page is different: a short **written list** of directions that are **not** implemented yet.

## Planned / not yet in the mod

The items below describe **direction**, not shipping promises. Order and final design can change.

- **Civilization map overlay** — Handheld map-style feedback that layers civilization information on the vanilla map (for example regional tinting) so territory reads at a glance.
- **Advancement track** — A structured progression path tied to discovering civ mechanics and structures.
- **Multiblock structures** — Larger built forms that act as gameplay anchors beyond single-block interactions.
- **Town center** — A hub block or structure for **managing and upgrading** civilization over a region, with a **custom GUI** and room for RPG-style branching upgrades over time.
- **Further-out ideas** — Factions, or hostile pressure **against** settled regions; if explored, they would be clearly separated from the core civ/spawn loop in documentation.

When a feature ships, it will gain a first-class wiki page under **Play** (or **Customize** if datapack-driven) and be removed from this list.
